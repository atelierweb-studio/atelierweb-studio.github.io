02 — Our story
We roast in small batches, twice a week, and sell what we can drink before it fades.

Loom & Roast started in 2019 with one drum roaster, a second-hand oven and a stubborn idea: a neighbourhood café should be able to tell you exactly where its beans came from and who picked them.

Today we work with four importers, roast on Tuesdays and Fridays, and bake every morning before the doors open. The room is small, the music is low, and the wifi is deliberately average.
