01 — The room

One room, kept simple.

Six single beds in a dormitory-style room with a private bathroom, fresh linen and furniture kept in good order. Rates are per bed, per night — write with your dates and we will confirm.

i1

Six-bed dormitory

Six single beds with individual bedding and reading lights. A group can book the whole room.

i2

Private bathroom

Inside the room, with hot water around the clock and towels provided.

i3

Lift from the platform

The building is connected to Hongik University Station by lift — no stairs with a suitcase.

i4

Airport limousine at the door

The Incheon Airport limousine stops in front of the building; the guesthouse is visible from the stop.
