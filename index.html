05 — FAQ

Questions before we start.

Short answers to what most people ask. Anything else, write to atlierwab@gmail.com.

Is 48 hours realistic?

For a one-page site with copy ready, yes. Multi-page projects usually land in four to five working days. The date is confirmed in writing before we start.

How does the language switch work?

Every page carries its own small dictionary of text. One tap rewrites headings, buttons, forms and even form messages in place — no reload, no separate subdomain, no third-party widget.

Who writes the translations?

We do a first pass in all six languages and keep the tone consistent. For regulated fields such as medical, legal or finance, we recommend a native reviewer before launch.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. You receive the source files and a one-page guide. Text for each page lives in a single block, so changing a price or a phone number is a one-line edit.

What about hosting and the domain?

We can deploy to your own host or a static host of your choice. Domain and hosting are billed directly to you — we never resell them.

Do you use templates or page builders?

No. Everything is hand-written HTML, CSS and a little plain JavaScript, so pages load fast and never break when a plugin updates.
