03 — Approach

Diagnose, decide, deliver.

No 40-page decks. A short diagnosis, one decision meeting, and then the work — with a named person accountable for each line.

01

Two-week diagnostic

Interviews, documents and a cost baseline. You receive a one-page finding and a scope with a fixed price.

02

Decision meeting

Ninety minutes with the people who can say yes. We agree what to do, what to skip and who owns each part.

03

Delivery with handover

Weekly written updates, then a handover pack so your team can run it without us.
